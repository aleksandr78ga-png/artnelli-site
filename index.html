Useful before ordering

A few clear answers

If your question is not here, write directly — we will help you choose the right format.

How do I order a ready-to-ship item?

Open the product card and send its name or Telegram publication link. We will confirm availability, measurements and delivery.

How does a custom order begin?

Tell us the discipline, music, colours and deadline. We discuss the concept, confirm the design and measurements, and only then begin tailoring.

Can measurements be taken remotely?

Yes. Use the illustrated PDF guide on this page and send the measurements for confirmation before tailoring.

Do you create costumes for teams?

Yes. Tell us the number of athletes, discipline, event date and desired visual concept so we can discuss the project.

How are timelines and delivery calculated?

They depend on the costume, workload and destination. We confirm the schedule and delivery option individually before work begins.
